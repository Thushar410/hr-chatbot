SECTION 1: PAID TIME OFF (PTO)
Full-time employees accrue 15 days of PTO per calendar year, accrued monthly
at a rate of 1.25 days per month. PTO must be requested at least 5 business
days in advance through the HR portal, except in cases of illness or
emergency. Unused PTO up to 5 days may be carried over into the next
calendar year; any excess is forfeited on December 31.

SECTION 2: REMOTE WORK POLICY
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are reviewed
annually. Remote employees must be reachable during core hours, 10:00 AM to
3:00 PM in their local time zone.

SECTION 3: HEALTH AND BENEFITS
New employees become eligible for medical, dental, and vision coverage on
the first day of the month following 30 days of employment. Acme Corp
covers 80% of the employee premium and 60% of dependent premiums. Open
enrollment occurs every November for coverage starting the following
January 1.

SECTION 4: ONBOARDING CHECKLIST (FIRST 2 WEEKS)
1. Complete I-9 and W-4 forms in the HR portal before your start date.
2. IT will provision your laptop and accounts on day 1.
3. Attend new-hire orientation (day 1, 10:00 AM, virtual).
4. Complete mandatory compliance training within your first 2 weeks
   (Code of Conduct, Anti-Harassment, Data Security).
5. Schedule a 30-day check-in with your manager.
6. Enroll in benefits within 30 days of your start date or you will need to
   wait until the next open enrollment period.

SECTION 5: EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
Business expenses must be submitted through the expense portal within 30
days of the purchase, with an itemized receipt attached. Reimbursements
are processed on the next bi-weekly payroll cycle after approval.

SECTION 6: WORKPLACE CONDUCT AND REPORTING
Acme Corp has zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination of any kind.
Employees who experience or witness such conduct should contact Employee
Relations directly at employee-relations@acme-corp.example or use the
anonymous ethics hotline. This assistant does not handle these reports —
it will always direct you to a human in these cases.
